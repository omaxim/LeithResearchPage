What we do

Machine learning for building efficiency

Most buildings have never had an energy assessment. We train models on the ones that have, then estimate energy use and efficiency for every building in a country, with honest uncertainty. The results feed retrofit targeting, levy design and national policy costings.

Climate scenarios, assets and transition risk

Connecting IPCC and IEA scenarios to real power plants, factories and company revenues: scenario pipelines, capture-price and cash-flow models, and asset-level datasets that let banks and supervisors see where transition risk actually sits.

Trade and industrial-policy analysis

Product-level trade data turned into export-opportunity maps and green-complexity indicators, so ministries can see where a country's clean-tech strengths really are and where to place support.

3.9Mbuildings

Energy efficiency of every building in the Czech Republic

Only a fraction of Czech buildings hold an energy performance certificate. We trained a model on the 262,000 that do and estimated energy use and an efficiency class for the other 3.7 million, with a full probability distribution for each building rather than a single guess. The national picture it produces is now used in policy costings.

0.8×the average price

Transition risk at asset level with Theia Finance Labs

Climate scenarios give one average electricity price per country, but a solar farm doesn't earn it and a gas plant earns more. We support Theia Finance Labs with the data and models that turn scenarios into asset-level revenues: capture-price ratios, scenario-to-asset pipelines and global facility datasets.
